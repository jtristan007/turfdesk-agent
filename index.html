Turfdesk AI Agent

Your business runs itself
while you're in the field

An AI agent built into Turfdesk that handles invoice follow-ups, client onboarding, and daily health checks — automatically, every day.

Runs every morning at 7 AM
How it works

Three jobs. Every day. Zero effort.

Once set up, the agent connects directly to your Turfdesk account, checks what needs attention, and takes action — sending emails, onboarding new clients, and flagging anything that needs your eye.

💸
Job 1

Invoice Reminders

Finds every overdue invoice across all your clients. Groups them by company and fires off a professional reminder email to each owner — so you get paid without having to chase anyone.

  • Detects overdue invoices automatically
  • Groups by client, not per-invoice
  • Sends branded reminder email
🤝
Job 2

New Client Onboarding

When a new business signs up, the agent instantly sets up their service menu (lawn care + snow removal) and sends them a welcome email with their first three steps.

  • Seeds 12+ default services (summer & winter)
  • Sends personalized welcome email
  • Marks account ready in seconds
📊
Job 3

Daily Health Report

Pulls a platform-wide snapshot and emails it to you: active companies, revenue at risk, critically overdue invoices, and invoices sent that day. Green or red — you know the moment you wake up.

  • Active companies count
  • Revenue at risk (overdue total)
  • Flags invoices 30+ days overdue
A typical morning

What happens at 7 AM

You're doing a site walk. The agent runs quietly in the background.

7:00 AM — Agent wakes up

Connects to your Turfdesk database and starts Job 1.

7:01 AM — Overdue invoices scanned

Marks any invoices past their due date. Groups them by company. Sends reminder emails to owners who owe money.

7:02 AM — New signups checked

Any business that joined in the last 24 hours gets their services seeded and a welcome email in their inbox.

7:03 AM — Health report sent to you

You get one email with the full picture: everything green, or a red flag if something needs your attention.

Done — back to your day

No logins. No chasing. No manual emails. It just runs.

Getting started

What you need to run it

The agent is a Python script that connects to your existing Turfdesk Supabase database. Setup takes about 20 minutes.

1. Get your API keys

You need an Anthropic API key (from console.anthropic.com) and your Supabase service role key.

2. Set up email sending

Point the agent at any SMTP server — Gmail, Postmark, SendGrid, or your existing email provider.

3. Schedule it (optional but recommended)

Add one line to cron (0 7 * * * python turfdesk_agent.py) and it runs every morning at 7 AM.

4. That's it

The agent connects, runs all three jobs, and sends you the daily report. Nothing else to do.
